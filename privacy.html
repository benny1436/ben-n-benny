Privacy Policy for Helvite

Last Updated: May 8, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at Helvite. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us.

When you use our mobile application, as the case may be (the "App"), you trust us with your personal information. We take your privacy very seriously. In this privacy notice, we seek to explain to you in the clearest way possible what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it.

1. Information We Collect

Locally Stored Health Data

Helvite is designed as an offline-first, private health tracker. All health vitals and personal metrics you log into the App-including but not limited to heart rate, blood pressure, sleep duration, weight, steps, and menstrual cycles-are stored strictly locally on your device.

Profile Information

Any profile customizations, such as your name, gender, and profile picture, are also saved exclusively on your device's internal storage.

We do not transmit, sync, upload, or backup any of your health or profile data to external servers, cloud databases, or third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data you input is used solely within the App to provide you with its core functionalities, such as:

  • Visualizing your health trends and analytics over time.
  • Generating personalized health reports (PDF/Images) that you may explicitly choose to export or share.
  • Providing a personalized dashboard experience based on your tracked vitals.

3. Will Your Information Be Shared With Anyone?

No. Because all data remains on your device, we (the developers) do not have access to your data. Consequently, we do not-and cannot-sell, rent, or share your personal health information with third parties, advertisers, or analytics services.

If you choose to use the "Share Activity" or "Export Report" features, you are utilizing the native sharing capabilities of your device's operating system. You are fully in control of who receives your health reports.

4. Third-Party Permissions

The App may request the following permissions to function correctly:

  • Photo Library / Storage Access: To allow you to pick a profile picture or save generated health report images to your gallery.

We only access these resources when you initiate an action that requires them, and we do not use these permissions to collect data in the background.

5. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

Your information is kept for as long as you keep the App installed on your device or until you manually delete your records within the App. Uninstalling the App or clearing its application data via your device settings will permanently delete all your logged health data.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Since we do not collect or store your data on any servers, you have full control over your data directly within the App. You can:

  • View all your logged health records.
  • Edit or delete individual vital entries.
  • Completely wipe your data by uninstalling the application.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this notice, you may contact the developer directly.
